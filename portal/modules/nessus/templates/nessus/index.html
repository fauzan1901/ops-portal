Overview

The vulnerabilities Tenable found on the management networks of every site with its Nessus scanner, kept by the portal: what is open, what came back, what was fixed in the last 30 days, and how it went since the first sync.

Findings

On site . One row per vulnerability (plugin) with the hosts that have it; open a row for its hosts, or show every finding. A finding is Reopened when Tenable found again what was fixed.

Hosts

On site . Every address of the management networks with findings, the worst first. A host opens its findings with the output of the scanner and the solution.
